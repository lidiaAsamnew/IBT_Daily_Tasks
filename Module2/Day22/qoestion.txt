Work these in order. Each is small and testable on its own before you move to the next. 
1. Scaffold index.html with empty containers (status, convert form, result, an empty currency select, and an empty watchlist), then declare the state object in app.js.
2. Write render() against fake hard-coded rates (e.g. { USD: 0.0177, KES: 2.29 }) and confirm the dropdown fills before you touch the network.
3. Replace the fake data with loadRates(): fetch the live endpoint, check res.ok, store data.rates into state, and handle the loading and error messages. 
4. Wire the convert form: preventDefault, read and validate the amount with Number(), look up the rate from state, and show a formatted result line. 
5. Add the watchlist: an add button (guard against duplicates), renderWatchlist() from state, and one delegated click listener to remove a row by its data-c.
6. Add save() and load() with localStorage and call them from init(); reload the page and confirm your watchlist is still there.
